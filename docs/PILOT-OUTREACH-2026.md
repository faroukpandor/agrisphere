## 1. The three priority conversations (in order)

### Conversation A — Export chain: BMC/MIRA ecosystem or export abattoir
- **Goal:** first paid EUDR-readiness workspace (pilot) + permission to run
  a live demo with a real supply-chain group.
- **Card:** use the one-pager (OFFER doc). Emphasise: Dec-2026 calendar,
  supplier-level data gap, co-op aggregation model already built, free for
  their ranchers.
- **Ask:** named pilot lead + one real chain (e.g. a group of ranches
  supplying one abattoir line) + 60-minute checkpoint monthly.
- **LOI scope (fill into OFFTake-MOU template where a purchase element
  exists, otherwise use this sheet):**
  - Pilot term: 12 weeks from signature.
  - Deliverables: hosted workspace; holding records for the pilot group;
    self-declared consignment exports in the buyer's required layout.
  - Data: farmer-declared, consent-first; partner gets read/export access
    only to records shared with it (AgriSphere enforces this).
  - No exclusivity, no minimum commitment beyond the pilot.
  - Decision point: week 12 → paid licence (price envelope in the offer).

### Conversation B — Co-op / union workspace (BAMCU-type)
- **Goal:** one co-op running pooled export lots using shared member
  holdings; validates the aggregation story (EUDR + Temo Letlotlo both want
  collective bargaining & plot-level origin).
- **Card:** show the co-op flow in the demo: member shares holding with the
  co-op account → co-op builds a lot chain → exports file.
- **Ask:** one real member group (10–30 producers) for the pilot; their
  secretary/leader as the workspace admin.
- **Value to them:** free forever for members; strengthens their position in
  Temo Letlotlo collective-bargaining conversations; export-grade records
  for any buyer they approach.
- **LOI scope:** data-sharing rules per member (revocable), leader roles,
  no fee for members during pilot; fee only from the co-op if it on-sells
  export services.

### Conversation C — Insurer or programme partner (Pula-type / BAMB)
- **Goal:** evidence-kit pilot — planting/geo/photo records used as claims
  evidence for index insurance; and/or BAMB bulletin partnership (below).
- **Card:** insurer side: they need low-cost onboarding + claims evidence;
  we already store timestamped, geolocated, consent-first records and
  export evidence files.
- **Ask:** their claims template + a sandbox of 5–10 anonymised records to
  prove the export fits their workflow.
- **Honesty guard:** AgriSphere never underwrites, never shares data without
  consent, and will not be presented as an "insurer recommendation".

## 4. MoA / Temo Letlotlo complement briefing (internal one-pager)

**Context (from RESEARCH-2026-REFRESH):** Temo Letlotlo (replacing ISPAAD)
is output-based assistance with seasonal guaranteed loans/insurance,
automated digital registration, and collective bargaining. The Ministry
(and parastatals like BAMB, MIRA, BMC) is digitising around it.

**AgriSphere's complement — not a competitor, no subsidy capture:**
1. **Verified farmer profiles + records** that producers can attach to
   applications/collective-bargaining asks (consent-first; producer owns
   the record).
2. **Co-op workspace** output/aggregation records that match the
   collective-bargaining structure Temo Letlotlo describes.
3. **Geo-trace/EUDR layer** for the beef chain MIRA/BMC coordinate —
   export-grade records from the same producer data.
4. **Bulletin partnership** (BAMB letter above) and knowledge-base
   alignment with the programme's crops/insurance messaging.
5. **No pay-to-play, no data selling, farmers free forever** — the terms
   AgriSphere can put in writing for any public-sector conversation.

**Meeting ask:** 30 minutes with the digital-agriculture desk to
demonstrate and to ask where AgriSphere data could *save the programme
work* (e.g. verified producer records for registration checks). [owner:
identify the actual desk/name through the Ministry directory — do not
guess contacts.]

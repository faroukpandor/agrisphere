Grow with AgriSphere — four partnership tracks.

Whether you are a buyer building a reliable supply chain, a farm stay welcoming guests, an organisation that wants its own branded assistant, or a funder amplifying farmer support — there is a structured, transparent track for you.

Free for individual farmers, always. Commercial partners fund the platform through fair, labelled arrangements — see docs/MONETISATION.md.

How we work together

  • Transparency: every commercial arrangement is written (templates below), labelled to users, and consistent with the ethics & compliance framework.
  • Fairness by design: buyer programmes must pass the fair-terms checklist; tourism listings carry safety notes; no pay-to-play answers, ever.
  • No money handling: AgriSphere connects & documents — payments, bookings and contracts sit between you and your counterpart.
  • Value for farmers: partners expand market access, experiences and services that make the platform more useful — that is the deal.

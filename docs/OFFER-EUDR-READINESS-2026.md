## 1. The ask in one sentence
**From December 2026, every consignment of Botswana beef/cattle placed on the
EU market must carry due-diligence data showing exactly where the animals
were kept — we put that data on a durable, exportable record for your
supply chain, at farmer/co-op level, in weeks not years.**

## 2. Why now (the calendar)

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| **30 Dec 2026** | Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (as amended by 2025/2650) applies to large/medium operators placing cattle/beef on the EU market — geolocation of every holding where animals were kept, per consignment |
| **30 Jun 2027** | Same duty for micro/small operators |
| Rolling | Botswana's practical position depends on EC implementing acts (SADC EPA context) — re-checked quarterly in our research cycle |

Every EU-facing buyer will be asking suppliers *"where is your geo-trace?"*
before the first deadline. First movers set the format their suppliers use.

## 3. What AgriSphere does
A working, consent-first **geo-trace documentation layer** (live now on the
platform, demo available):

1. **Holdings on record** — each ranch/feedlot/pasture with coordinates,
   recorded **only with the owner's explicit consent** (consent timestamp
   stored); owner controls exactly which accounts may use a holding
   (co-op / exporter sharing model, revocable).
2. **Consignment lots with holding chains** — each lot of animals linked to
   the chain of holdings it passed through, in date order, with no gaps and
   no overlaps (enforced).
3. **Self-declared readiness export** (CSV or JSON) — per-consignment file
   with holding names, coordinates, periods, buyer and destination market,
   ready to attach to due-diligence work or hand to a buyer's system.
4. **Runs beside your existing chain** — free for farmers at the edge;
   buyers/exporters/co-ops pay for the workspace, batches and export files.

## 4. What it deliberately is not (our honesty rules)
- ❌ Not a certificate. ❌ Not a TRACES due-diligence statement.
- ❌ Not a deforestation-risk/legality assessment — that stays with the
  operator and competent authorities (we say so inside every export file).
- ❌ Not AI-vision or magic — records are farmer/rancher-declared, and the
  platform's trust rules (verified identities, honest ratings, audit log)
  are what make them usable.

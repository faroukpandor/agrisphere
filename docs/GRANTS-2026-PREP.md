# Grants & Programme Capital — 2026 Preparation Pack (STRATEGY §9, Days 31–90)

> Evidence base: the Africa agritech equity market shrank to ~US$168M in
> 2025 (vs US$776M in 2022) and is DFI/donor-anchored — **grants and
> government/parastatal service contracts are AgriSphere's realistic
> non-revenue capital path** (RESEARCH-2026-REFRESH §3.1). This pack
> prepares proposals in advance so that when a window opens the response
> time is days, not weeks.
>
> Honesty rules for every application (non-negotiable):
> - Claim only what the platform demonstrably does (public code, `/trust`
>   live counters, 167-assertion suite, self-declared-limits framing).
> - Never promise certification, compliance guarantees or diagnosis
>   accuracy; never inflate user numbers (there are none yet — pilots are
>   the honest metric).
> - Budget only real, defensible costs; state co-funding honestly.
> - If a window's rules exclude early-stage/unregistered entities, respect
>   that — the entity step unlocks most of them.

## 3. Standard proposal skeleton (draft once, adapt per window)
1. **One-liner:** AgriSphere is Botswana's farmer-owned-data platform —
   free multi-channel agri-assistant (Web/USSD/WhatsApp), verified
   commerce, and the country's first consent-first geo-trace layer for
   export-ready chains (EUDR timelines).
2. **Problem:** input advice, market asymmetry, and zero supplier-level
   traceability infrastructure as EU rules bite from Dec 2026.
3. **Response (what we will do with the grant):**
   a. Scale the assistant + co-op workspaces to N pilots
      (`[N from window scope]`);
   b. Take the geo-trace layer to M export-chain producers (real lots
      exported, the honest success metric);
   c. Train/publish evidence kits with a licensed insurer partner;
   d. Open data: knowledge base + trust page stay public.
4. **Beneficiaries:** farmers free forever; orgs pay — grant money buys
   reach and evidence, not free-forever farmer features (those already
   exist).
5. **Metrics (honest):** engaged producers & completed documents, lots
   exported with complete chains, unanswered-queue turnaround,
   opt-out/erasure rates — never vanity registrations.
6. **Workplan:** 3 phases × 6 months, matching pilot checkpoints.
7. **Budget:** see §4.
8. **Team & governance:** owner/operator + adviser board; governance log
   public; external review each milestone.
9. **Sustainability:** B2B2F revenue (workspaces, export services,
   programme licences) — grant de-risks, doesn't subsidise forever.
10. **Annexes:** audit, compliance, research docs, LOI(s), letter from
    pilot partner.

## 5. Per-window prep checklist (run when a call opens)
1. [ ] Re-read the window's eligibility (entity, geography, TRL stage) —
   if not eligible, do not apply (reputation is capital).
2. [ ] Fill skeleton §1–10 into the window's form; keep their language,
   our facts.
3. [ ] Pull live `/trust` numbers the day of submission (dated screenshot
   in the annex).
4. [ ] Two reviewers: one domain (agronomy/compliance), one for hype —
   delete every word that overclaims.
5. [ ] Budget arithmetic re-checked against quotes; exchange-rate caveat
   noted.
6. [ ] Submit; log date/window/amount in the governance log; track
   outcome and use feedback in the next attempt.

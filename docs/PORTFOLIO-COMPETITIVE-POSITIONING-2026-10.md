# AgriSphere — Portfolio Position and Consolidation Decision

**Date:** 16 October 2026 | **Status:** analysis only.

AgriSphere is currently described only minimally in its README, while AgriSage360 is the designated agricultural intelligence platform and AgriSolutions the physical execution layer. Do not build overlapping functionality here until the repository's actual code, ownership, current users and unique capabilities are audited.

## Competitive landscape to inform reuse decisions
- Local platforms: ReKamoso AgriMart (horticulture marketplace; livestock/poultry stated to be in build), Agrima Farmers (ministry-linked listings/import licensing; status needs re-verification), AFA (marketplace/bookkeeping/finance connections), Lima-Thuwa Connect (reported internal testing in April 2026), PulaFeed (broad ecosystem description; operations unverified).
- Inputs: Yeno Agric, Growfast, OFMB and conventional input suppliers; compare only with labels, local availability, quotes and independently validated trials.
- Farm management: Farmbrite (offline records, finance, livestock/crops, traceability), FarmERP (enterprise operations and contract farming), farmOS (open-source reference).
- Market/value-chain: Freebird Poultry, AgroCenta, Twiga Foods, AFEX and Hello Tractor as distinct models, not all direct competitors.

## Decision
Keep this repo in reference/legacy status until a code and capability audit identifies a unique, validated role. Reuse only after checking licence, provenance, data contracts and ownership. Canonical roles remain AgriSage360 (intelligence), AgriSolutions (execution), SmartFarm (farm system of record), AnimalHealth (animal-health domain), Research Exchange (research), and Everything City/AgriLink (commerce only if canonical ownership/status is verified).
